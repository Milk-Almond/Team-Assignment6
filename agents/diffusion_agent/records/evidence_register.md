# Dated evidence register for ChatGPT case

Reviewed October 27, 2026 (America/New_York). These original-source pages establish bounded observations, not a census of comparable organizational workflows.

| ID | Original source and date | Observation and correct denominator | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E1 | [Pew Research Center, June 17, 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/); survey February 17–23, 2026, n=5,119 US adults | 44% said they had ever used ChatGPT; 38% of employed adults used AI chatbots for tasks at work | Ever-use is not regular ChatGPT work use; work item covers other chatbots; 2026 questionnaire differs from earlier years. No firm or workflow denominator. |
| E2 | [OpenAI, September 2026](https://openai.com/index/the-work-now-within-reach/) | Company reports more than one billion weekly product users and 2.5 million businesses across its products | First-party global product reach; business count spans products and is not a fraction of all firms or evidence of this exact workflow. |
| E3 | [OpenAI, November 5, 2025](https://openai.com/index/1-million-businesses-putting-ai-to-work/) | More than 1 million paying business customers includes ChatGPT for Work **or API**; more than 7 million ChatGPT for Work seats | Historical vendor count, mixed products, seats not active people; cannot be combined into a peer adoption rate. |
| E4 | [OpenAI enterprise report, December 2025](https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/the-state-of-enterprise-ai-2025-report/) | About 9,000 surveyed workers in almost 100 OpenAI customer enterprises; Enterprise message volume increased about 8x over a year | Customer-selected, not representative of all firms; messages measure use intensity, not successful human-reviewed drafting or outcomes. |

**Weak claim withheld:** “ChatGPT has reached the early majority for human-reviewed internal document drafting at comparable US firms.” E1–E4 provide no representative firm-level denominator for sustained use of this exact task. Neither a weekly user count nor a paid seat count supplies it. Absence of a rate is not evidence that no peers use it.

**Evidence gap:** No named organization, industry, workflow baseline, local controls, peer cohort or measured quality outcomes were supplied. The cases below are explicitly hypothetical. No standard, opinion leader or change agent was shown to cause this application's diffusion. Product distribution and paid business tooling are observable ecosystem conditions; their effect on comparable peer use is inference.
