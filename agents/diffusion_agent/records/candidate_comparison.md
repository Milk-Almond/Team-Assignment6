# Candidate comparison and synthesis rationale

Reviewed the five linked individual repositories at their stated branch/default branch on October 27, 2026. Their cases differ; the shared team case is ChatGPT, so individual case-specific evidence is not transferred as proof of ChatGPT adoption.

| Candidate | Case and useful design contribution | Limitation retained for synthesis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Meilin Pan](https://github.com/mp7240-sudo/MASY1800-Emerging-Technology-Diffusion-Agent/tree/assignment-5-diffusion) | Wealth intake; careful source populations, conflicting sector signals, preserved revision and raw responses | Adjacent advisor tools do not show client-facing intake prevalence; posture-specific timing remained weak. |
| [Shenghao Ma](https://github.com/Harry-max264/Lab5) | Procurement; application evidence ladder, denominator warnings and provenance of constructed negative fixture | Vendor pilot and roadmap do not demonstrate sustained industrial peer diffusion. |
| [Yulia Anisimova](https://github.com/yanisimova/SubAgent-Diffusion) | Coding assistant; three posture contrasts with stable external evidence and explicit factor map | Survey use or intent does not establish exact task or firm-level adopter stage. Record's branch/commit field was not updated. |
| [yicrry777](https://github.com/yicrry777/1800lab5) | Theranos; sharp separation of service reach from proprietary platform diffusion and historical hindsight | Retrospective clinical case cannot supply present ChatGPT evidence or a workplace timing conclusion. |
| [sawulya](https://github.com/sawulya/Lab-5---SubAgent-4-Diffusion) | Banking chatbot; explicit rejection of generic chatbot counts as GenAI diffusion | Top-ten bank chatbot use has wrong construct and denominator for GenAI support. |

**Team synthesis choice:** Carry forward the shared three-level scaffold, strict construct/denominator/date test, five factor mapping and posture contrast. Use a new ChatGPT evidence register and common test. The strongest alternative interpretation is that broad use and growing business seats justify a confident early-majority label. We reject that label **for the specified workflow**, because the cited units do not count comparable firms performing it routinely. Broad individual uptake remains a strong direction-of-spread signal.

All team members approved the integrated SubAgent 4 Diffusion Agent, Agent Record, tests, and Adoption Agent handoff on October 27, 2026. This approval applies to the team synthesis described above; the retained limitations and dissent record remain part of the approved artifact.
