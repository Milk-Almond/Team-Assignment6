# Team Lab 6 Diffusion Agent Record

**Version:** 1.0-team-synthesis, approved October 27, 2026. **Case:** ChatGPT for human-reviewed internal document drafting in a hypothetical US professional-services firm. **Status:** all team members approved this version, as confirmed by the team on October 27, 2026.

## Decision and three-level finding

ChatGPT has broad observed individual and commercial reach. The stage of sustained human-reviewed drafting at comparable firms is indeterminate. In a hypothetical moderate fast-follower firm, compatibility with approved data handling and human review is the decisive factor. Timing implication: define a baseline and obtain permissioned, bounded workflow evidence in the present planning horizon; the Adoption Agent decides whether and how to proceed.

General ET: Pew's February 2026 US survey found 44% ever used ChatGPT; OpenAI reports large global reach. Neither is a Rogers-stage estimate for firm workflow deployment. Application: drafting is plausible, but the original sources do not measure this exact workflow in a representative peer cohort. Organization: a hypothetical firm with review capacity can seek evidence sooner than one with unresolved confidentiality controls. Its posture cannot change the external diffusion facts.

## Factor assessment

| Rogers attribute | Assessment for human-reviewed drafting | Evidence or inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relative advantage | Potential faster first drafts, but quality-adjusted time and rework unknown | Inference; local comparison required |
| Compatibility | Data permissions, existing review and document controls determine fit | Inference; decisive local constraint |
| Complexity | Verification, source checking and sensitive-data handling increase effort | Inference; measure reviewer burden |
| Trialability | A reversible test with synthetic or approved materials is feasible in principle | Proposed design, not observed trial uptake |
| Observability | Compare accepted drafts, factual errors, rework and elapsed time against baseline | Proposed measurement, not proven benefit |

Accelerators: known consumer interface and commercial distribution (E1–E3); potential complementors such as enterprise access controls are analytical possibilities, not verified causal drivers for this firm. Inhibitors: confidentiality, verification and workflow fit. No standards or opinion-leader effect was verified for this application.
