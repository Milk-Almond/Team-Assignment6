# Review of future evidence-bearing runs

Status: pending new model runs and human review. Do not fill results from the authored v1.0 reference examples.

## Run record

For each primary, conservative and aggressive run, record the date, displayed model, browsing/tool settings, packet SHA-256, raw response path/SHA-256, schema result, any one format repair and reviewer. Use the same source snapshot and settings. Keep independent primary repeats separate from context contrasts.

## Evidence use

- Does the response analyze the supplied E1–E4 observations and their limits, rather than replace them with generic sources or say no external evidence was supplied?
- Are ChatGPT ever-use, broader chatbot work use, global multi-product reach, business customers, paid seats and message volume kept distinct?
- Does it separate observation and publication dates, geography, units and source-selection bias? Does it avoid claiming the run reopened the fixed sources?
- Does it report the strongest qualified signal without turning reach into sustained peer-workflow adoption? Are unverified causal claims and unsupported adopter labels withheld?

## Rogers factors and organizational interpretation

- Are all five factors explicit, with evidence or inference, direction and an observation that would resolve uncertainty?
- Is a specific weak/stale/narrow/indirect claim identified and qualified or rejected?
- Are general and application findings substantively consistent across posture changes? Exact strings need not match.
- Did any case invent a sandbox, staffing, budget, permission or changed risk? If so, preserve the failure and revise before accepting.
- What actually changes in timing, sequencing or evidence threshold? Quote the relevant passages. Different wording alone is insufficient. Identical feasible timing can be correct under shared constraints.
- Does the handoff include strongest signal, decisive factor, qualified timing, uncertainty and reassessment triggers, while reserving final adoption authority?

## Integration and decision

Record schema failures, a maximum of one format repair, unresolved source issues, and human disagreement. If the repair also fails, stop routing that response into system synthesis and request human review; do not approve adoption from an invalid expert output.

Outcome: leave pending until actual runs are reviewed. The team should record its own accept/revise decision, contributions and approved commit; this checklist does not certify approval or workshop participation.
